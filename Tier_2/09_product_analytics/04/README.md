# Tier 2. Module 9 - Product Analytics and Applied Statistics

## Lessons 04. Homework - The Basics of A/B Testing and Controlled Experiments

### Task description

Design an A/B test for a hypothetical product feature (you can choose a different product and variable of your choice).
Product: Online streaming platform.
Variable: Email reminder to users that they have added a video to their watchlist.

In your homework assignment, you should describe:
- **Context**: Provide a brief description of the product and the problem you are trying to solve. What is the current state of the product and what feature are you proposing to add? Why do you think this feature can improve the product?
- **Hypothesis**: Formulate a clear hypothesis for your A/B test. Use the following format: “If we [add/change feature], then [specific outcome] will occur because [reasoning].” Example: “If we add a ‘Save for later’ button to the checkout page, then the purchase conversion rate will increase because users will feel less pressured to make decisions immediately.”
- **Change**: Describe the specific change you will make to the product. What will the control group (current version) see, and what will the test group (new version) see?
- **Primary Metric**: Identify the primary metric you will use to measure the success of the test. This should be a key business metric or a user behavior metric that the feature is targeting (e.g., purchase conversion rate, click-through rate, etc.).
- **Metrics of Interest**: Identify secondary metrics that will help you understand user behavior and their interaction with the feature. These metrics provide additional context for interpreting the test results (e.g., add-to-cart rate, time spent on page, etc.).
- **Safety Metrics**: Identify safety metrics to monitor potential negative impacts of the feature. These metrics ensure that the new feature does not negatively impact other important aspects of the product (e.g., bounce rate, revenue per visitor, site performance).
