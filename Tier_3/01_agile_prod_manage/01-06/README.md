# Tier 3. Module 1 - Agile Product Management for Software Development Teams

## Lessons 01-06. Homeworks - Business Model Canvas. Unit Economics

### Task description

In this homework assignment, you will practice creating a Business Model Canvas and calculate unit economics for your product.

Building a Business Model Canvas for a new product helps you to understand in detail how the business will function, how it will make money, and also improve the value proposition, determine the necessary resources and partners to implement the business idea, identify its potential risks and vulnerabilities, develop strategies for improving and managing them.

Calculating unit economics allows you to determine whether you will be able to make money, how much you can spend on marketing and advertising, what price will allow you to achieve the desired level of profitability, make decisions about business development, investments and attracting investors.

So, today you will:

- write down the 9 blocks of the Business Model Canvas for your product;
- find benchmark metrics;
- determine CAC, LTV, ROMI for your product and draw conclusions.

#### Homework Description

You will continue working in teams with the selected product in the previously made copy of the [template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/150cuql5ylRWz2qudCzSi58f4EN6GPsrw/edit#gid=1068647246) in the folder with your stream number.

The task consists of two stages:

- Stage 1 - filling out the Business Model Canvas for your product.
- Stage 2 - researching benchmarks and calculating CAC, LTV, ROMI for your product based on them.
