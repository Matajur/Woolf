#### Required blocks

**Project name (introduction)**

- Describe your product in one sentence.

**Problem**

- Describe the problem that your product or service solves, and for whom.

**Solution**

- How your product solves the problem.
- Communicate the Product Vision and Value Proposition of your business. Announce your big idea — what you do better than others.

**Market size and opportunities**

- Indicate the CAGR and market volume in monetary terms, also add a link to the source.
- Calculate your expected market share.
- Describe your target audience through Personas.

**Product**

- Indicate your USP for your product: how specifically you are different from others and how specifically you help personas.
- Describe your MVP concept, basic functionality.
- Indicate the capabilities of your product or service.
- Create the first prototype using improvised means (drawings, videos, collages, etc.).

**Competition**

- Indicate the results of the analysis of your main competitors.
- Indicate your competitive advantages.

**Business model and finances**

- Describe how you plan to make money: selling a product, subscriptions, advertising, providing services.
- Indicate how you plan to promote yourself in the market and find new customers.
- Add tables with unit economics: how much you plan to earn, spend, and, if there are calculations, when your project will pay off.

**Investments and use of funds**

- How much money is needed and for what.

**Team**

- Tell us about the team members, their relevant experience, and their roles.

4. Design your slides from a design perspective

#### Basic rules for designing your slides:

- **Colors. Contrast.** Use your own color scheme, but it should be clear and attractive. Use a contrasting background and font. On a dark background, a font size of up to 18 pt and a line spacing of up to 1.3 are allowed.
- **Text.** The text on the slide should not duplicate the speaker’s text. The slides are not overloaded with text.
- **Images.** Use only those that visualize your idea. Don’t add images for fun. Avoid white men and poor-quality stock photos. Images can be found on free resources [unsplash.com](http://unsplash.com/), [pixabay.com](http://pixabay.com/) and paid ones — [depositphotos.com](http://depositphotos.com/), [shutterstock.com](http://shutterstock.com/). To avoid pixelation, the photo should be in FullHD — 1920×1080.
